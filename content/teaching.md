## Astana IT University

*Assistant Professor, 2024–2025*

* **Stochastic Processes with Applications to Finance.** Core concepts from random variables to stochastic processes, including martingales and filtrations. Advanced topics such as the Feynman–Kac formula, change of numeraire, Radon–Nikodym derivatives, and Girsanov's theorem, with applications to option pricing. Implied volatility, FFT-based pricing methods, and Monte Carlo simulation for vanilla and exotic derivatives, with emphasis on dynamic hedging.
* **Computational Geometry and Spline Methods in Finance.** B-splines and NURBS, linking computational geometry with financial modeling. Spline-based collocation methods for PDE pricing problems, and spline approximations of characteristic functions in SDE settings as an alternative to COS methods.
* **Finite Element Methods in Mathematical Finance.** Numerical methods for PDE-based option pricing, focusing on implementation, stability, convergence analysis, and practical financial applications.
* **Calculus I & II.** Undergraduate courses covering limits, differentiation, integration, and multivariable calculus, emphasizing both theoretical understanding and applied problem-solving.
